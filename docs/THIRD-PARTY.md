# Third-party components

CivilBIM uses PdfPig 0.1.9 and its UglyToad.PdfPig assemblies for local PDF reading.

- Source: https://github.com/UglyToad/PdfPig
- Package: https://www.nuget.org/packages/PdfPig/0.1.9
- License: Apache-2.0. See PdfPig-LICENSE.txt and PdfPig-NOTICES.txt.

Autodesk Revit and AutoCAD are separately installed products. Their API/runtime DLLs are not redistributed in this package.

NetTopologySuite 2.6.0 is used for polygon unions, nearest-borehole cells and terrain intersections. BSD-3-Clause; see NetTopologySuite-LICENSE.md including upstream notices. Source: https://github.com/NetTopologySuite/NetTopologySuite/tree/v2.6.0
